01 / Research areas

Research areas

01

Computer graphics

CG

3D graphics, animation and visual expression

＋

We explore visual expression through computer graphics, studying techniques and creating work to investigate new possibilities.

Works & activities↗Discuss this research area↗
02

Drones / UAV

Development, control and flight testing

＋

We study the development and application of reliable, agile drones, including flight testing, low-latency video processing and autonomous flight.

Works & activities↗Discuss this research area↗
03

Extended reality

Experiences and application development

＋

We develop VR applications and explore educational uses of platforms such as VRChat, drone-based VR applications, and connections between the web and VR.

Works & activities↗Discuss this research area↗
04

Film / Video

Filming, editing and motion graphics

＋

We work on techniques for visual storytelling, expression through new technologies, and collaborative film and video production.

Works & activities↗Discuss this research area↗
05

Media art

Light, sound and spatial expression

＋

We create technology-based expression through projection mapping, laser light, and combinations of music and moving images.

Works & activities↗Discuss this research area↗
06

Generative AI

Video production and new approaches to problems

＋

We investigate video production using generative AI and the development of new approaches to solving problems.
